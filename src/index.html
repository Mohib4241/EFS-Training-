HOW A TRAINING SESSION WORKS

Learn it. Run it. Maintain it.

Every machine follows the same four steps, so a new starter always knows what comes next.

  1. 01

    Explore

    Open the machine out in 3D. Point at any part to see its name and what it does.

  2. 02

    Operate

    Connect, switch on and watch it work: dust into the bag, water into the tank.

  3. 03

    Practise

    Watch a demonstration of the maintenance task, then do each step yourself.

  4. 04

    Check

    Find the visible defect and choose the safe response: keep it out of use and report it.
